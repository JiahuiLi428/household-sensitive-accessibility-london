# Extension results summary

The full 100m grid contains 157370 cells; OSM residential filtering retained 106018 residential-origin cells.

Caregiving-adult centroid-grid Pearson correlation is 0.85 for walk-only and 0.845 for walk+public transport.

Lowest-accessibility quintile Jaccard overlap for caregiving adults is 0.454 in the walk-only scenario, showing that centroid and grid-informed approaches identify overlapping but not identical low-access areas.

Grid-informed high-high mismatch counts are: children/adolescents 780, caregiving adults 677, and older adults 883.

Compound mismatch identifies 576 two-group and 134 three-group high-high mismatch LSOAs.

The confirmed case studies are Barking Riverside / Thames View, Hillingdon 003D as the compound mismatch case, and Barking and Dagenham 015F as the positive counter-case.
